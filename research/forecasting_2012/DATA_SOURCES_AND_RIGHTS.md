# Source attribution and reuse

The principal source is **Lahmeyer International / Ministry of Water Resources and Electricity, Republic of Sudan (2012)**, *Long and Medium Term Power System Plans — Demand Forecast Report, WP II and WP III, FINAL Revision*, report **260690**, **18 October 2012**. The supplied PDF has 139 pages; its SHA256 appears in `source_metadata.json`. Page references use physical PDF pages because printed page numbering restarts.

The public collection contains normalized factual datasets, original extraction/analysis code, original charts and numerical checks. The PDF, full-text transcription, original report figures and raw page-table exports are not included. The original report carries a copyright notice; this repository does not grant permission to reproduce that report or claim it has an open-data licence.

The repository MIT licence applies to original project software. It does not relicense underlying third-party material. Users should preserve the source references, consult the original source's terms for their intended reuse and cite both the source and this repository revision.

`source_register.csv` and `SOURCE_REGISTER.md` identify the original data owners cited by the report, including CBS, SEDCo, SETCo, MED/MWRE, IMF and UN-HABITAT. A related public link is not a claim that the exact utility workbook has been recovered. The separately labelled 2019 implementation check cites the World Bank diagnostic and is not evidence of present asset condition.

These are historical observations and planning assumptions of different vintages. The data are not a verified 2022 fleet inventory or a post-war damage assessment. `results/source_issues.csv` records known inconsistencies. See the notebook and data dictionary before using them in a model.

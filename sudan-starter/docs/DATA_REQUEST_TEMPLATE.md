# Utility/research-partner data request template

**Draft only. Reference period: January–December 2022. Fill in your name, affiliation and recipient before sending.**

Subject: Research collaboration and electricity-system data request for PyPSA-Sudan

I am developing an open historical model of Sudan's electricity system using PyPSA, with calendar year 2022 as the reference period. The first goal is to reconstruct and validate generation, demand and transmission before studying future options. I would appreciate collaboration on validating the system representation and, where permitted, access to the following data for January–December 2022. National UNSD statistics provide an initial benchmark, but unit-level and network records are still needed.

| Dataset | Requested fields and units |
|---|---|
| Plant/unit register | Stable plant/unit ID; name; public location or supply region; technology and fuel; gross/net installed MW; dependable MW; commissioning/retirement dates; dated operating status; heat rate or efficiency |
| Generation | Hourly or daily output MW/MWh by unit; monthly MWh; outages, maintenance and reasons; start/ramp/minimum-output limits where available |
| Demand and supply | Hourly MW and monthly MWh by substation/region; timestamp convention; metering boundary; distribution/transmission losses; unserved load estimates separated from served load |
| Transmission | Public/releasable single-line topology; bus IDs; voltage kV; line endpoints; length km; circuits; R/X and units; normal/emergency MVA; transformer ratings; dated outage status |
| Hydro | Daily/monthly inflows and units; reservoir levels/volumes; turbine capacity; power/flow relationship; environmental/irrigation releases; spill and energy output |
| Imports | Hourly/monthly flows; usable and firm MW; outage dates; purchase costs and price basis |
| Isolated/distributed supply | Diesel/PV/battery capacity by region; fuel availability; captive generation; service hours and estimated demand |
| Costs | Fuel and transport price/currency/date; variable/fixed O&M; rehabilitation costs; investment costs; component lifetime |

Please indicate definitions, quality flags, missing values, revisions, source dates and units. If detailed data cannot be shared, aggregated regional/monthly values or engineering review of the preliminary model would still be valuable. Please specify which data may be published, which require aggregation, and which must remain private. We can use public identifiers and exclude sensitive operational detail from the public repository.

I would welcome discussion of scientific collaboration and appropriate attribution for data and expertise. I can share the preliminary methodology, editable input tables and list of unresolved assumptions for review.

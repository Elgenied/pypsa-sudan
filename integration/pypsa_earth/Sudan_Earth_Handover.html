PyPSA-Sudan → PyPSA-Earth: the evidence handover¶

Status: prepared inputs and tested interfaces, not a newly solved national model.

We are adopting the Zambia-style organisation: a Sudan evidence/configuration layer on top of the standard national PyPSA-Earth workflow, plus a complementary OnSSET electrification analysis. We do not need to reproduce every earlier prototype inside PyPSA-Earth.

The initial national configuration uses Sudan, full-year 2022, ten spatial clusters and three-hour chronological averages. Ten clusters are not ten states or ten settlement zones. OnSSET's access focus remains Northern, River Nile, Khartoum, Gezira, White Nile and Sennar.

Read the integration guide for WSL commands and the full evidence map. This notebook can be rerun without downloading data or solving a network.

1. The demand that actually enters the model¶

The source statistic is 13,261 GWh of end-use consumption (including an UNSD estimate). The initial sent-out boundary also includes 3,652 GWh of aggregate technical/nontechnical losses. These losses must not be added a second time.

The hourly shape is synthetic. It has been shifted from local UTC+2 to UTC as a periodic annual template. source: gegis in the YAML selects the standard file reader; it does not make this a GEGIS forecast.

The 882 GWh import benchmark is not yet an import generator or border link in the new Earth model. This is a remaining baseline calibration task.

2. Plant evidence: useful, but not a complete 2022 fleet¶

The crosswalk preserves all 32 inventory rows, including planned entries. Eleven entries can be linked explicitly to coordinates in the older engineering report. Planned-only projects are excluded from the Earth-format candidate file.

The candidate file is disabled. Historical nameplate MW, national net MW, reported available MW and future project MW are different quantities. Garri 4's provisional fuel-carrier mapping and hydro reservoir classifications also require review. Earth retains its default fleet until corrections are reconciled. The displayed locations are dated report coordinates, not newly surveyed sites.

3. How post-conflict evidence will enter¶

Evidence Model treatment What it cannot establish
Fuel-limited plant Available fuel/dispatch constraint in a dated scenario Destroyed capacity or repair bill
Reported plant shutdown Scenario availability with documented cause A measured year-round outage probability
Network separation Explicit outage of mapped lines/transformers Which asset failed without topology matching
Rehabilitation option Defined restored capacity, cost and lifetime The same thing as standard new-line expansion
DRE security category Historical exposure/context Current damage, access safety or a financial risk probability
Local equipment quotes Documented cost components after harmonisation Utility-scale turnkey costs in the same price year

The three agreed recovery conditions remain limited, targeted and broad restoration. We first need a common baseline. Keep assumptions explicit; do not convert each report statement into another elaborate scenario.

Standard Earth expansion does not automatically optimize rehabilitation. If rehabilitation choices are fixed outside the optimization, call them recovery scenarios, rather than claiming the optimizer selected repairs.

5. The next concrete work¶

  1. Configure Copernicus access privately in WSL and obtain the full 2022 ERA5 cutout; standard geography and resource data must also be retrieved.
  2. Build the Earth network, inspect its actual buses/lines and reconcile the plant fleet, hydro and 882 GWh import boundary.
  3. Run and check the fixed-fleet baseline against the national statistics.
  4. Add the three recovery conditions with traceable asset availability and rehabilitation costs; enable generation/storage/transmission investments as appropriate for that experiment.
  5. Run complementary OnSSET access cases and present both branches in the main research notebook.

Do not interpret a successful dry-run as a validated dispatch result. The full execution commands and evidence provenance are in README.md and the manifest.

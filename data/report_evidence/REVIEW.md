# What the reports add, and what to do with it

Source IDs resolve to filenames, titles, hashes and links in [source_register.csv](source_register.csv). Page references below are **physical PDF pages starting at 1**. Printed pages can differ, particularly in the CSP manuscript, UNDP reports and two-page annual-report spreads.

## What can now be done

**The immediate next model should be a small, transparent service-option comparison**, followed by a national rollout only after the inputs and constraints survive review.

1. **Reconcile the asset reference.** Match the historical plant groups to the 2022 inventory by technology and physical unit, retaining separate nameplate/net/available capacity. Keep rental and autoproducer scope explicit. Do not simply replace 2022 with the 2019 or 2024 annex total.
2. **Build a dated recovery case.** Use the August 2024 status evidence as one documented scenario. Create separate assumptions for fuel delivery, repair eligibility/cost/time, workforce and corridor availability. Keep unobserved quantities unknown until a transparent scenario assumption is chosen.
3. **Prepare comparable local costs.** Use the dated battery quotations as evidence; confirm the panel rating and quote unit. Obtain a matching SDG/USD rate and a complete bill of quantities: PV, inverter kW, battery nominal/usable kWh, installation, civil works, transport, protection, metering, distribution, connections, replacement and maintenance. Specify one constant-price base year. Keep grants/financing and economic resource costs separate.
4. **Select a small set of real DRE clusters.** Include near-grid, distant/compact and distant/dispersed cases; add at least one irrigation or clinic load. Match service requirements across grid extension, mini-grid and standalone options. The DRE demand formula is a starting proxy, not a measured service requirement.
5. **Run the comparison.** PyPSA can optimize generation, storage and interconnection for defined candidate systems. Network routing, settlement assignment and standalone/mini-grid/grid choice need explicit candidate costs and decision logic. PyPSA-Distribution or MicroGridsPy may supply a detailed local submodel; neither fills missing costs, demand or damage data automatically.
6. **Test robustness.** Vary demand, diesel availability/price, grid return date, installation cost, real discount rate, battery replacement and reliability. Validate selected systems against independent site data or another implementation. Only then extend to all clusters and publish a conditional least-cost option map.

This extraction has not run steps 1-6. It supplies traceable evidence for them.

## Still missing for credible technology-choice maps

| Missing item | Why these PDFs do not close it | Best next source/action |
|---|---|---|
| Current usable MV/LV routes and substations | Aggregate lengths and schematic maps lack topology, ratings and condition | Request SEDC/utility GIS and dated asset condition; use surveyed pilot routes |
| Repair/rebuild quantities, costs and durations | “Unavailable” seldom identifies damage class or repair scope | Engineer-reviewed inventory and bills of quantities |
| Full installed equipment and network costs | Retail/import quotes omit technical scope, delivery and installation | Comparable local quotations, dated FX, explicit VAT/duties treatment |
| Settlement access and actual households | Buildings/nightlights do not prove service; survey counts require weights | Household/enterprise surveys, metering and local verification |
| Hourly critical/productive/residential loads | Annual sales and irrigation outputs are not chronological demand | Pilot measurements or appliance/crop-based profiles with documented assumptions |
| Solar/wind chronology and hydro operating limits | Annual potentials and TMY aggregates omit correlations and dry years | Common weather years, resource conversion and hydro inflow/operational records |
| Battery and inverter design | Nominal kWh alone lacks usable fraction, kW, efficiency, warranty and degradation | Manufacturer specifications and procurement quotes |
| Independent validation | Transcription and arithmetic establish consistency, not truth | Utility/local experts, independent datasets and benchmark systems |

The claim supported now is an **evidence-informed scenario study under explicit assumptions**. These reports do not yet support a verified reconstruction schedule or an investment-ready national design.
